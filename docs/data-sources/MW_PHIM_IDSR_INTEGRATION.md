# Malawi PHIM IDSR weekly bulletins

Official sources: [PHIM bulletin posts](https://phim.health.gov.mw/wp-json/wp/v2/search?search=IDSR&per_page=100&page=1) and the [PHIM media catalogue](https://phim.health.gov.mw/wp-json/wp/v2/media?search=IDSR&per_page=100&page=1). Reviewed 2026-11-16 against original 2025 weeks 7, 13, 24, 25 and 37 and 2026 weeks 17, 21 and 43 PDFs. This is an internal source-native ingestion contract, not a public-release approval.

- Country: `MW`; source scope: `mw_phim_idsr_weekly`; ontology source: `SRC_MW_PHIM_IDSR`.
- Official PDF files are discovered from bulletin posts and the paginated official media catalogue, not constructed from week numbers or URL slugs. The media catalogue recovers post pages without PDF links and broken post PDF links; both original and replacement URLs remain in the audit.
- Collector: `src/services/ingestion/countries/mw.py`; parser: `src/data/crawlers/mw.py`; updater: `src/data/processors/mw.py`.
- Stored observation: explicit national Table 1 suspected cases and, where printed, deaths, indexed by the reporting year and epidemiological week printed on the PDF cover. The date is the Sunday ending that week. Raw disease labels and PDF SHA-256 remain on every observation.
- The early 2025 Table 1 prints only a `Suspected cases` column. Those bulletins contribute case observations only; the absent death column is not represented as zero. The January 2025 media upload for 2024 week 50 is excluded from the 2025-onward source window by its PDF cover.
- Source zeroes remain zero. A missing PDF, unsupported table, split/ambiguous cell, figure-only value and missing week do not create zero observations. Publication date is separate from the observation period.
- Cumulative outbreak narratives, Annex district/hospital tables, and incident EBS signals remain out of the case series until their individual reporting contracts are reviewed. Malaria is not in Table 1, so this first parser does not claim national malaria coverage. Conflicts between same-week PDF editions are withheld and audited.
- Some 2026 reports put a case number below its row label in the PDF text stream. The parser reads these only when the PDF page has exactly one numeric cell in the observed case column and one in the death column for that row. Ambiguous or incomplete cells remain unparsed with a report-level audit entry.
- The official report may disagree across prose, figures and Table 1. Table 1 supplies the canonical facts for this source scope; disagreements remain a source-quality concern and must not be reconciled by guesswork.
- Original PDFs, post pages, search JSON and media-catalogue JSON are persisted as durable source artifacts. A 24-hour integrity-checked transport cache avoids repeated downloads of unchanged files while allowing routine revalidation. A forced run re-fetches from the source.
- `public_release_enabled=False`; reuse terms and generated public snapshot remain unverified.

Known sample checks: the week 17, 2026 PDF has 255 suspected cholera cases and 0 deaths in Table 1; week 37, 2025 has 718 reported diarrheal-with-blood cases and 42 SARI cases. The week 43, 2026 SARI case/death cells extract in a reversed line order; their verified PDF columns establish 53 cases and 0 deaths.

Run the focused source tests with `venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/unit/test_mw_phim_idsr.py`. `scripts/import_mw_history.py` can execute a queued task under a fenced foreground lease or replay a completed task's captured originals offline. Runtime imports should inspect `source_audit` for discovered reports, captured originals, parsed tables, failed downloads and conflicting observations. Country publication requires separate disease mapping, rights review and snapshot acceptance.
